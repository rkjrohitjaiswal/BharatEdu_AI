# BharatEdu AI - 3-Minute Live Hackathon Pitch

**Target Duration:** 3 Minutes  
**Audience:** Hackathon Jury & Panel

---

## Pitch Script Breakdown

### 0:00–0:30 — The Problem (Equitable Education Access)
> *"Over 30 million children in India face learning loss or drop out of school due to a lack of personalized guidance, language barriers, and economic hardship. Existing AI tools often fail because they provide ungrounded, hallucinated answers or operate purely in English."*

### 0:30–1:00 — The Solution (BharatEdu AI)
> *"Meet BharatEdu AI—a grounded, multilingual, equitable education platform. We combine NCERT-grounded RAG doubt solving, adaptive practice, automated learning gap detection, teacher intervention analytics, and official scholarship matching into one seamless system."*

### 1:00–2:15 — Live Product Demo
> 1. **AI Tutor + RAG**: *"Watch us ask a doubt in Hindi. BharatEdu AI answers in Hindi and attaches the exact NCERT Class 8 page citation—zero hallucinations."*
> 2. **Adaptive Practice & Gap Engine**: *"When the student takes a practice session, the system evaluates their answers server-side, detects a prerequisite algebra gap, and steps up difficulty automatically."*
> 3. **Teacher Intelligence**: *"On the Teacher Dashboard, teachers immediately see at-risk students flagged with targeted intervention recommendations."*
> 4. **Scholarship Matching**: *"Finally, in Scholarships, the student sees a 100% potential match for NMMSS with document checklists and a direct link to scholarships.gov.in."*

### 2:15–2:40 — Tech Stack & Differentiators
> *"Built with React 18, Node.js, TypeScript, and MongoDB. Key differentiators: 100% deterministic risk/mastery calculations, server-side answer security, native Web Speech voice input in English, Hindi, and Gujarati, and zero client-side secret exposure."*

### 2:40–3:00 — Impact & Vision
> *"BharatEdu AI ensures no child drops out due to lack of academic support or financial awareness. Thank you!"*
